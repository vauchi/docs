**Version 1.1 — October 2026**

Thank you for contributing to Vauchi. Before we can merge your
first contribution, we ask you to agree to this Contributor
License Agreement ("Agreement"). It follows the model of the
Apache Software Foundation's Individual Contributor License
Agreement.

## Why we ask

Vauchi is free software and stays free software: everything we
publish is available under an open source license. The Agreement
does two things on top of that:

- It confirms that you have the right to contribute what you
  send us.
- It lets the Licensor also offer Vauchi under other license
  terms — for example a commercial license to an organization
  that cannot accept the AGPL — and change the open source
  license in future (as Vauchi did when core moved from the GPL
  to the AGPL). This keeps the project able to fund itself
  without closing any code.

**You keep the copyright in your contribution.** The Agreement is
a license, not a transfer of ownership.

## How to sign

Sign once, before your first contribution is merged:

1. Add one line to the
   [signature list](./cla-signatures.md) in the `vauchi/docs`
   repository, in a merge request opened from the account you
   contribute with:

   ```text
   | Your Name | @your-gitlab-username | 2026-10-24 | 1.1 |
   ```

2. In that merge request's description, write:
   "I have read the Vauchi Contributor License Agreement version
   1.1 and agree to its terms."

The commits in that merge request are your signature. If you
contribute from more than one account or email address, list
them in the same merge request.
